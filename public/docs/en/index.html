Start in ten minutes

Your store works the moment Fawh is installed: all 30 home blocks are placed with sample text and photos, and every setting starts at the best choice for a perfume store. These steps make it yours.

  1. Activate the theme from Store Design in your Salla dashboard, then open Customise to reach the theme editor.
  2. Upload your logo in Store identity, on a transparent background (PNG), and set its size in Header › Logo size.
  3. Pick your colours in Identity, colours and fonts: Fawh bronze, your Salla store colour, or your own.
  4. Replace the hero photos and text in Home page › Fawh · Hero slider.
  5. Arrange the blocks: drag to reorder and remove what you do not need. You can add any block back later.
  6. Set up your menus in Store › Menus in Salla, with a photo for each section.
  7. Write your perfume notes in each product description to show the notes pyramid (see Product page).
Tip

Promo banners stay empty until you add them, so shoppers never see an empty frame while you set up.

Colours, fonts and dark mode

Fawh ships with warm bronze colours made for perfume. Switch to your own brand colour, or give shoppers a full dark mode with one tap.

WhereTheme settings›Identity, colours and fonts

  1. Choose Store colours: Fawh colours (default), your Salla store colour, or Custom to set the main colour, backgrounds, text and sale colours.
  2. Pick a body font and a heading font. All are Arabic fonts with Latin letters: IBM Plex, Tajawal, Almarai and more; headings in Amiri, El Messiri or Ruqaa.
  3. Under Dark mode choose off, a header button that each shopper sets for their device, or always dark. Then choose what a first visit shows: light, dark, or the device setting.
  4. Optionally give dark mode its own colours.
Tip

The mode is set before the page paints, so there is no white flash when the store opens dark. Colours set in a block’s Section design take priority over store colours.

Header, menu and search

The header is the first thing shoppers see. Fawh gives you three layouts, a mega menu with section photos and products, and a search window that suggests before anyone types.

WhereTheme settings›Header

  • Three layouts: floating over the hero, classic with a search field, or a side menu button. Plus logo position and transparency over the hero.
  • Sticky header: always (default), smart (hides on the way down, returns on the way up), or off.
  • Mega menu: hovering a section shows its subsections with photos, its products and its banner. Sections that do not fit move into a More button.
  • Announcement bar above the header: scrolling or rotating messages, five designs, a highlighted button, and a countdown to the end of an offer.

Search window

WhereTheme settings›Header›Search

  1. Set the trending words title and source: your menu sections automatically, or up to 12 words you order yourself, each running a search or opening a category or link, with a “trending” flame on one or two.
  2. Choose the suggested products: best sellers, picked products, newest in a category, or deepest discounts, 2 to 8 of them.
  3. Optionally add a featured product or banner beside them.
Tip

Link a trending word to a category rather than a search: a word that matches no product names shows no results.

Built for phones

Most perfume shoppers buy on their phones. Fawh is designed for them first, from 320-pixel screens up.

WhereTheme settings›Mobile

  • A floating bottom bar with the buttons you choose (home, sections, search, cart, account) and their labels.
  • A side menu with round section photos at every level, a photo you upload for the offers, blog and brands links, and a banner.
  • Product rows show one card with the edge of the next, so shoppers know the row swipes. Section tabs split the screen width evenly.
  • A filter tab on the screen edge on category pages, shown only beside the products.
Tip

Change cards per phone screen in each block’s Display settings. We recommend one for products, two or three for sections and small images.

Home page blocks

Thirty blocks designed for perfume stores. Add, remove and reorder any of them; each opens in the editor with a card that explains it and a preview.

WhereTheme editor›Home page

  1. Click Add block and pick a Fawh block.
  2. Fill in its content: title, photos, products or categories.
  3. Choose its Display: a swipeable carousel, a grid, or a grid on computers and a carousel on phones, plus items per screen on each device.
  4. Open Section design at the end of the block to change its background, colours, width, corners and spacing.
  5. Save, and drag the block into place.

Section design

  • Background: a colour, a two-colour gradient with direction, or a photo per screen with dimming.
  • Colours: light or dark text, and colours for title, text and buttons.
  • Width and shape: a card within the page or full-bleed, corners, shadow and border.
  • Spacing and title: space above and below, title alignment, size and font.
  • Anchor: such as #offers, so a hero button can jump to the section.
Tip

While “Customise section design” is off the block keeps Fawh’s own design, so feel free to experiment.

Product page and notes pyramid

The Fawh product page sells a scent the way a good shop assistant does: its notes, how long it lasts, its brand, and the fewest steps to buy.

Notes pyramid

Write one line per stage in the product description and Fawh draws it as a pyramid, with timings and bars for longevity and sillage:

Top notes: bergamot, cardamom
Heart notes: Taif rose, jasmine
Base notes: oud, amber, musk
Longevity: 4
Sillage: 3
  • Alternative headings such as “Head notes” are understood, with :, - or | as the separator.
  • For a timing on one product, put it in brackets: Top notes (first 30 minutes): cardamom.
  • These lines are removed from the description tab so they do not repeat.

On the page

WhereTheme settings›Product page

  • Options as buttons instead of a dropdown for size and concentration; price and stock work as usual.
  • A brand card with its logo and a shop-the-brand link, plus an authenticity badge and perks.
  • Description position: a tab, an excerpt under the price, or in full under the price.
  • Sticky buy bar: once shoppers scroll past the buy buttons, a bar slides in with the photo, price, quantity and add button, plus Buy now and Apple Pay when your store has them. Choose its screens, look and parts.
  • Similar products and recently viewed rows with your own titles and display.

Product card

WhereTheme settings›Product card

  • Hover motion: a gentle zoom, the product’s second photo, a light sweep, or a lifted card.
  • Quick view: a window with photos, price, notes, quantity and add to cart, without leaving the page.
  • Compare: up to 4 products side by side by price, rating, notes, longevity and sillage.

Categories and offers

Category pages open with the category banner, then its subcategories in arches, then the products with clear filters.

WhereTheme settings›Category pages

  • Banner: within the page or full width, its height, dimming and text position. Its photo is the category photo in Salla.
  • Subcategories: each arch shows the category photo or its newest product, and empty ones show a fallback you choose: the first letter, the parent photo, your image or your logo.
  • Products: per-row count on each device, grid or list view, and perks above them.
  • Offers page: a banner with its photo, text and button, and filters you choose: categories, brands, discount, price ranges, in stock only.
  • Shop by budget: a page per price range, opened from the budget block cards.

Promo banners and popups

Show the right offer in the right place: an oud banner on oud pages only, a welcome popup for first visits, and a stronger offer for those who close it.

Promo banners

WhereTheme settings›Promo banners

  1. Add a banner and choose where: product page, category page, offers, cart, blog, info pages, account, login window, or side menu.
  2. Choose who sees it: everyone, or specific products, categories or brands. A parent category includes its children and their products.
  3. Upload the image for each screen, write the text and link, and choose the look: text over the image, image only, or image beside text.
  4. Optionally set start and end dates so the banner appears and disappears on its own.

Popups

WhereTheme settings›Coupon and popups

  • Audience: everyone, first visit, returning, guests, signed-in customers, or people who closed another popup. That builds a sequence: a welcome, then a stronger offer.
  • Where and when: pages and device; immediately, after seconds, after scrolling, or on exit intent. No popup ever shows in the cart or checkout.
  • Design: image beside or above the text, a discount stamp, a copyable coupon code, a button and link, and colours.
Tip

Changing the campaign name shows the popup to everyone again, even those who chose not to see it. Use it when you launch a new offer.

Blog, loyalty and brands

  • Brands: a page of all brands with an A–Z index, and a page per brand with its products.

Common questions

Do I lose my settings when the theme updates?

No. Updates reach your store and your settings, images and text stay as they are.

Does the theme work in English?

Yes. Every theme text is translated, and every settings field takes text per store language.

Why are section photos missing from the menu?

Upload a photo for each item in Store › Menus in Salla. Without one, Fawh shows the section’s newest product.

Why is the notes pyramid not showing?

Write the notes in the product description in the format shown in Product page, one line per stage.

Can I try the theme before buying?

Yes, with Preview in the Salla theme store, or on the live demo at fawh.vercel.app.
