# Changelog

## 0.9.1

- Notes section explains each stage, when it appears and what the two meters mean
- Buy now on the product page
- Coupon ticket opens only when the pointer rests on its stub; narrower stub

## 0.9.0

- Hero product section with options, quantity, add to cart and buy now
- Products with options open a quick options dialog wherever they are added from
- Listing pages: one wide or two banners, benefits strip, customer reviews; the sub-category row opens on the one being viewed; switch-style filters
- Call button above WhatsApp; back to top moved to the other edge
- Video player: previous / next, progress on the playing item

## 0.8.0

- Discounts show wherever a product appears (hero, video, reels, search, menus, reviews, sets)
- Welcome popup for new visitors with remind-later and don't-show-again
- Discount ticket on the screen edge: slides out on hover, copies the code on press
- Announcement bar restyled; slim scrollbar without track or arrows
- Home: collection banners redesigned, plain product section, pick your moment, gift card
- Recently viewed on the home and product pages, with a clear button

## 0.7.1

- Header menu opens and switches only after the pointer rests; content fades in; calmer, roomier layout

## 0.7.0

- Header menu rebuilt: full-width panel with a scrolling sub-category list, hover content (nested sections or products) and banner; closes on leave, Escape, link click and navigation
- Oud oil now has ten sub-categories, three of them nested, as a real example
- Reels, occasions and shop-by-note sections redesigned
