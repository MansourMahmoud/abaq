Every photo and video in `public/media` is stock material. This file records
where each one came from, so its licence can be checked before the store or
its screenshots are published anywhere new.

## Licences

- **Photos**: [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Free for
  commercial use, modification allowed, no credit required.
- **Videos**: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Free for
  commercial use, modification allowed, no credit required.

Neither licence covers what appears *inside* a picture. Trademarks, product
designs and recognisable people keep their own rights, and neither licence
allows suggesting that a person or brand endorses the store.
